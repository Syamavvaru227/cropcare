The photograph depicts a tomato plant; it is not represented as an Indian location or a disease sample. Bundled locally for offline use.

Disease entries are starter educational summaries, not the user's original database. Every entry is marked unverified. The linked University of Minnesota diagnostic guide is further reading, not evidence that the application was validated by agricultural experts. Disease illustrations are symbolic, not diagnostic photographs. Add expert-reviewed Telugu disease names and appropriately licensed disease-specific photos before public agricultural use.
